## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Audio:** Use trending TikTok sounds. For Option A, something punchy/energetic. For Option B, something chill and feel-good. Avoid copyrighted music for background — use royalty-free or TikTok's library.
- **Pacing:** TikTok attention spans are short. Cuts every 2-3 seconds max.
- **Text overlays:** Use TikTok's native text tool. Bold, large, centered. Animate with pop-in effect.
- **Screen recordings:** Film with phone screen recording. Use dark mode. Speed up scrolling sections 1.5x.
- **Face-to-camera:** Natural lighting, casual setting (bedroom, desk). Not over-produced.
